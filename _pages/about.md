I am an M.Eng. student in Instrument Science and Technology at Tianjin University (expected graduation: Jun. 2028), advised by Prof. Xiaodong Zhang. I received my B.Eng. in Measurement and Control Technology and Instruments from Tianjin University in 2025.

My previous work has focused on measurement-driven error diagnosis and closed-loop compensation in ultraprecision optical manufacturing. I aim to generalize this framework to advanced manufacturing and intelligent inspection by integrating in-situ metrology, physical process modeling, adaptive control, and agent-enabled engineering automation.

My projects span ultra-precision diamond turning of single-crystal germanium microlens arrays, coherence scanning interferometer probe development, and optical-performance-guided closed-loop compensation manufacturing. I am the first author of a review on coherence scanning interferometric measurement of advanced optics (Optics & Laser Technology, 2026) and a co-author of two further papers on wafer measurement and inspection.

**Research interests:** Ultra-precision optical manufacturing · Single-point diamond turning · Measurement-driven error diagnosis and compensation · Coherence scanning interferometry · In-situ metrology and intelligent inspection · Adaptive control and agent-enabled engineering automation
