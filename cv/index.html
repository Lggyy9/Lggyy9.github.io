Professional Summary

My previous work has focused on measurement-driven error diagnosis and closed-loop compensation in ultraprecision optical manufacturing. I aim to generalize this framework to advanced manufacturing and intelligent inspection by integrating in-situ metrology, physical process modeling, adaptive control, and agent-enabled engineering automation.

  • 2025 - 2026
    A closed-loop compensatory manufacturing process for monolithic multi-mirror systems guided by optical performance
    Lab-Initiated Research Project, Dec. 2025 – Aug. 2026
    • Led the experimental implementation and metrology of a closed-loop compensation process for a monolithic dual-reflector optical system manufactured by single-point diamond turning.
    • Developed and executed a Zemax ZOS-API workflow to simulate the propagation of measured surface errors and evaluate candidate compensation strategies in terms of system-level optical performance.
    • Processed and analyzed machining, surface-metrology, and optical-performance data to evaluate the effectiveness of the compensation method. Reduced the system wavefront error RMS by 90% after compensation iteration, experimentally validating the effectiveness of the optical-performance-guided compensation framework.
    • Consolidated the simulation and experimental results and led the preparation of the resulting co-first-author manuscript.
  • 2026 - 2026
    Compensation Single-Point Diamond Turning of Large Aperture Biconic Optical Surfaces
    Industry-Facing Engineering Project, Apr. 2026 – Jul. 2026
    • Led the diagnosis of systematic surface-form errors in the production machining of biconic optical components and established corresponding compensation strategies.
    • Developed feature-specific surface-error fitting algorithms for different error morphologies, enabling accurate representation of measured form errors with low fitting residuals.
    • Generated compensated tool paths for biconic surfaces with varying design parameters and supported their implementation in ultra-precision diamond-turning production.
    • Developed the core dynamic-link libraries for error-source identification, surface-error fitting, and compensated tool-path generation, which were integrated into a production-oriented software system delivered for routine use by laboratory engineers.
  • 2024 - 2025
    Development of a Coherence Scanning Interferometry Probe for Optical Profilometry
    Industry-Sponsored Project, Nov. 2024 – Nov. 2025
    • Developed dynamic-link libraries for instrument control and software integration, including image acquisition, scanning control, topography reconstruction, data reprocessing, and surface-roughness evaluation.
    • Integrated the algorithmic and software components with the probe hardware and carried out hands-on testing, contributing to the successful delivery of a functional CSI probe for an optical profilometer.
    • Participated in the component selection of a compact CSI probe integrating broadband illumination, piezoelectric vertical scanning, an interferometric objective, and industrial-camera acquisition, building on the laboratory’s earlier hardware development.
    • Independently designed and conducted exploratory microsphere-assisted CSI experiments for enhanced lateral-resolution measurement and identified limitations in microsphere manipulation, experimental stability, and forward-model validation.
