  1. OLT
    Coherence scanning interferometric measurement of advanced optics: A review
    Guoyuan Lyu, Qian Liu, Zexiao Li, Weisheng Cheng, Junge Xu, and Xiaodong Zhang
    Optics & Laser Technology, 2026
    摘要 DOI BibTeX 出版社页面

    This review systematically examines the technological advancements, applications, advantages, and limitations of Coherence Scanning Interferometry (CSI) for high-precision, full-spatial-frequency measurement of advanced optical components, and outlines its future transition toward miniaturized, industrial-grade sensors for in-situ measurement and real-time feedback.

  2. OLE
    Key technologies of a FOUP-integrated spectral confocal module for wafer warpage measurement
    Weisheng Cheng, Shuangxiong Yin, Yongxu Xiang, Guoyuan Lyu, Jun Yang, Zexiao Li, and Xiaodong Zhang
    Optics and Lasers in Engineering, 2026
    摘要 DOI BibTeX 出版社页面

    This paper presents a FOUP-compatible rotating dual-probe modular confocal wafer measurement system, integrating polar-coordinate scanning, Zernike polynomial modeling, geometric error calibration and compensation, and gravity compensation to enable high-precision, high-throughput, inline measurement of wafer warpage and bow for advanced semiconductor manufacturing.

  3. MST
    Bionic-inspired intelligent detection of surface defects on semiconductor silicon wafers
    Weisheng Cheng, Zexiao Li, Juncheng Yu, Fuqi Sun, Guoyuan Lyu, and Xiaodong Zhang
    Measurement Science and Technology, 2026
    摘要 DOI BibTeX 出版社页面

    This paper proposes a bio-inspired intelligent automated optical inspection (AOI) method for silicon wafer surface defect detection, integrating visual perception mechanisms, a multi-degree-of-freedom biomimetic inspection system, and a self-attention-based neural network with multimodal data fusion to enhance inspection accuracy, robustness, and adaptability under varying production-line conditions.
